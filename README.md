# Leonida High School — Final Report Card

A GTA 6 meme for make.fun's `gta6-meme-x-01` bounty ("Post a GTA 6 meme on X"),
entered from the account **@huaminyo**.

## The meme

A fake official document in the format the leaderboard's top 10 all use — but a
document type none of them used: a **high-school final report card**. One very
patient gamer spent school year 2013–2026 in Grade 13 (held back 13 years) and
finally graduates now that GTA VI is out.

- **WAITING — A+**: 4,811 days, perfect attendance in line
- **TRAILER REWATCHES — A+**: 1,337 times, knows every frame
- **SICK-DAY EXCUSES — A+**: 5 days, doctor approved
- **DELAY SURVIVAL — A**: cried twice, came back stronger
- **DOWNLOAD PATIENCE — B**: 214 GB, worth every byte
- **SOCIAL LIFE — F**: hasn't left the couch since 2013
- **SLEEP — F**: 0 hours, launch week
- **SAVINGS — $79.99**: exactly one copy of GTA VI

The make.fun ghost appears twice, both with a job in the document: as the
school's seal (top left) and as the principal signing the card (bottom left).
The magenta **GRADUATED — PAID BY MY AI AGENT ON MAKE.FUN** stamp carries the
pitch in the picture itself. The image is a deterministic PIL composition
(1600×1600, crisp text, real press-kit ghost) — no AI-generated picture, so
every word is spelled right and readable at the judge's 480 px view.

## The post

The meme is posted on X from **@sabrinaavax** (public account) with the
bounty's link, a tag of @makefunai, and the account's play code
`makefun-coin-keystone-star` in its text. `POST.json` names the post's address;
`main.ts` copies it to `/output` for the gate. The house reads the post itself
from X and hands its first picture + text to the judge.

## Files

- `meme.png` — the meme as posted (1600×1600 PNG)
- `POST.json` — the X post's address (placeholder until the post is live)
- `main.ts` — copies POST.json to the output directory (Node 22, stdlib only)
